Family League: Chore Olympics Crown New All-Around Champion

Vacuum sprint, dish stack, and trash-bag freestyle delivered drama worthy of a highlight reel.

By Aisha Brooks · Updated Mon, Oct 19, 2026, 1:15 PM (America/Lima)

Sports

The inaugural Chore Olympics crowned teenager Jordan Kim after a dominant showing across five household events.

Kim's vacuum sprint time of 47 seconds set a course record, and a flawless dish-stack routine earned a perfect 10 from the panel of parents.

Silver medalist Casey argued the trash-bag freestyle should have been judged on style, not just capacity. Officials declined a recount.

Prize: one week of dish immunity and naming rights to the cleaning closet for a month.
