Family League: Underdog Cousins Stun Favorites in Thanksgiving Turkey Bowl

A last-second lateral and a muddy end zone sealed the upset of the decade in backyard football.

By Chris Delgado · Updated Wed, Nov 4, 2026, 4:20 PM (America/Lima)

Sports

In front of a packed audience of folding chairs and one confused dog, the Cousins United squad defeated defending champs Team Elders 21–20 on a trick play that will be debated until next Thanksgiving.

Quarterback Maya "No Look" Patel completed a behind-the-back lateral to her brother, who dove across the Cool Whip–stained goal line as time expired.

"Film study was just watching last year's group chat arguments," Patel said. "We knew they would bite on the fake handoff to Uncle Ray."

MVP honors went to the family golden retriever, who intercepted two passes and refused to return either football.
