Markets Plunge as Household Faces Unprecedented Grocery Budget Crisis

Analysts scramble after avocado prices and impulse snack buys push the weekly food fund into the red.

By Marcus Chen · Updated Sun, Nov 1, 2026, 11:00 AM (America/Lima)

Business

The Household Reserve Fund closed Friday down 27 percent after a midweek Costco run and an emergency ice-cream restock following a tough workday.

Chief financial officer (and roommate who does the spreadsheet) issued a rare public statement: "We remain solvent, but discretionary guacamole is under review."

Emergency measures include a three-day rice-and-beans austerity plan and a temporary ban on "just browsing" the snack aisle.

Markets are expected to stabilize once payday lands. Investors—namely parents who Venmo for birthdays—are watching closely.
