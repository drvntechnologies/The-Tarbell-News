Senate of the Household Passes Sweeping Laundry Reform Bill

A bipartisan coalition of roommates and visiting cousins approved new rules for the shared washer after a contentious floor debate.

By Priya Okonkwo · Updated Mon, Nov 2, 2026, 2:30 PM (America/Lima)

Politics of Life

In a narrow 3–2 vote, the Household Senate approved the Clean Clothes Accountability Act, mandating that wet laundry cannot remain in the machine for more than 45 minutes.

Supporters hailed the bill as overdue justice for those who have waited barefoot outside the laundry closet. Opponents warned of overreach and "sock surveillance."

The legislation also creates a detergent stipend and a whistleblower hotline—currently a group chat named "Washer Watch."

Implementation begins this weekend. Enforcement will be handled by whoever finds the wet load first.
