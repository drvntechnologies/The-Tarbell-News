Recap: Board Game Night Ends in Historic Upset—Monopoly Crown Dethroned

After three hours and one near-walkout, a dark-horse Catan player took the weekend hardware.

By Chris Delgado · Updated Mon, Oct 26, 2026, 10:40 PM (America/Lima)

Sports

The annual Game Night Championship, long dominated by Monopoly specialists, saw a seismic shift when wheat-and-ore strategist Lena Park closed out a Catan victory at 10:38 p.m.

Park's longest road cut through three rival settlements and left the former champ stranded on Baltic Avenue energy.

"I studied the robber meta all week," Park admitted. "Also I brought better snacks. That helped."

Organizers confirmed next season will include a formal replay review for disputed dice rolls—and a hard 11 p.m. curfew.
