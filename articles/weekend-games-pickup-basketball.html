Weekend Games: Pickup Basketball Crown Changes Hands After Triple-OT Thriller

The park court saw its longest game of the season as dusk forced a sudden-death finish.

By Aisha Brooks · Updated Sun, Nov 1, 2026, 7:05 PM (America/Lima)

Sports

What started as a friendly Saturday run became legend when neither side would yield through regulation and two overtimes.

With light fading and parents calling kids home, captains agreed to one final possession. Rookie guard Eli Torres drained a contested floater at the buzzer.

"My shoes are destroyed and I have never been happier," Torres said while icing both ankles on a cooler lid.

Next week's rematch is already scheduled. Trash talk has migrated from the court to three separate group chats.
